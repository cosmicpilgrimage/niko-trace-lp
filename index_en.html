What is Tracing

What is tracing?

Tracing is a simple, quiet practice — you follow the lines of a sacred script or sigil, one stroke at a time. No skill or calligraphy experience is needed; as you follow each line, your breath settles and your mind grows still. For centuries, copying sacred letters has been a form of prayer and purification, and these books bring that tradition into a simple daily ritual. Experiences vary — you only need to begin with a single stroke.
